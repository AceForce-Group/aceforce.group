The plan

History in BigQuery, daily work in Cin7 and QuickBooks

Loading 20 years of line-level detail into QuickBooks isn't realistic: it can only take it as hundreds of thousands of summary journal entries, and the item detail is lost. So the history goes to its own database, and Cin7 and QuickBooks start clean.
