01

Prestige haircare brand

A custom connector registered as its own Intuit app

  1. Cin7 Core
  2. AceForce connector

Starting point
The standard sync could not post DTC, gratis and wholesale orders the way the accountants needed.
What we built
Built a custom Cin7 Core to QuickBooks connector: full DTC sync including gratis, and wholesale cost of goods booked at shipment. Built the cutover report when the brand later switched to the native sync.
Result
  • Posting rules the native sync could not handle
  • A clean handoff between methods
