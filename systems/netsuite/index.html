Case studies

Transaction automation

01

Global beauty brand

Transfer orders that create themselves

  1. NetSuite item receipt
  2. AceForce automation
  3. Transfer order + fulfillment
Starting point
Moving inventory between locations meant building transfer orders and fulfillments by hand from purchase orders and receipts.
What we built
Built an hourly weekday integration triggered by a flag on the item receipt. It groups receipts, aggregates SKUs, creates the transfer order and item fulfillment, copies shipment, container and freight data, books an estimated landed cost and sends one email linking every record.
Result
  • Live in production after sign-off from accounting and logistics
  • The client came back for the next project: landed cost
"Now that we have wrapped up the Transfer Order automation, I'd like to start exploring our next potential project with Aceforce around our landed cost process."VP of Accounting, global beauty brand

EDI, 3PLs and vendors

04

Beauty brand, DTC and B2B

Retail EDI orders straight into NetSuite

  1. 3PL EDI
  2. AceForce connector
  3. NetSuite
Starting point
Wholesale EDI orders arrived at the 3PL and had to be keyed into NetSuite.
What we built
Built a scheduled weekday sync that pulls EDI orders into NetSuite as sales orders, then fulfills and invoices them. Mapped shipping cost by retailer, shipping income to the wholesale department, cancel dates and store numbers, and split multi ship-to purchase orders into separate sales orders.
Result
  • Accounting signed off on the mapping before go-live
  • Wholesale orders, fulfillments and invoices with no keying

Transfer orders that book the warehouse for you

  3. Capacity

Connector rescues

08

Beauty brand, DTC and B2B

51 stuck orders cleared in a week

  1. Shopify
  2. NetSuite Connector
  3. NetSuite
Starting point
Bundles sold below the sum of their components failed NetSuite's order total validation. 51 orders were blocked and the team got more than 100 error emails a day.
What we built
Traced it to an item group setting rather than prices, worked the case with Oracle and corrected nine item groups without touching standalone prices.
Result
  • All 51 orders posted
  • Zero DTC or B2B errors on the follow-up check
