Accounting and QuickBooks

Custom connectors and corrector scripts that keep Cin7 Core and QuickBooks honest.

01

Prestige haircare brand

A custom Cin7 Core to QuickBooks connector

  1. Cin7 Core
  2. AceForce connector
  3. QuickBooks Online
Starting point
The standard sync could not post the brand's mix of DTC, gratis and wholesale orders the way its accountants needed.
What we built
Built a custom connector registered as its own Intuit app. DTC orders, including gratis, synced in full. Wholesale orders booked cost of goods as a journal entry at the moment of shipment, not at invoice. When the brand later moved to the native integration, we built a Cin7 report that found every order caught between the two methods so finance could close the gap.
Result
  • DTC and wholesale posting rules the native sync could not handle
  • A clean cutover report when the brand changed methods
02

Oral care and fragrance brands

Reclass scripts that fix the accounting before it lands

  1. Shopify
  2. Cin7 Core
  3. AceForce reclass script
  4. QuickBooks
Starting point
Each Shopify order arrives in Cin7 Core as its own sale, and channel revenue was posting to the wrong accounts in QuickBooks.
What we built
Wrote accounting reclass scripts that run against Cin7 Core and move sales and cost of goods to the right channel accounts before they sync, built around the one order per Shopify order structure the brands rely on.
Result
  • Channel revenue and cost of goods land in the right accounts
  • Running in production for two brands
03

Streetwear brand

A corrector script and a full accounting reset

  1. Shopify x2
  2. Cin7 Core
  3. AceForce corrector script
  4. QuickBooks
  5. Bill.com
Starting point
Cin7 Core, two Shopify stores, QuickBooks and Bill.com disagreed, and the books could not be reconciled.
What we built
Mapped the Cin7 chart of accounts to QuickBooks, wrote a corrector script that reclasses revenue and cost of goods across channels, split source of truth between Bill.com and Cin7, set daily consolidation across both stores, added purchase invoice authorization and landed cost rules, audited account assignment SKU by SKU, and trained the team on the new production flow.
Result
  • One set of rules across two stores and four systems
  • Production purchase orders restructured so vendor bills stop arriving at zero
04

DTC fragrance brand

Cin7 Core and QuickBooks Online back in sync

  1. Cin7 Core
  2. QuickBooks Online
Starting point
Cin7 Core and QuickBooks Online were not reconciling, and finance could not trust either system.
What we built
Rebuilt invoice, cost of goods and credit note mappings, added an invoice authorization gate, cleaned up the integration end to end and handed over to the team and its outside accountants. Stayed on to reconnect QuickBooks when authorization lapsed and clear stuck orders.
Result
  • Stabilization finished in about five weeks
  • Ongoing support handled the next problems as they came up

Retail EDI orders that ship and invoice themselves

  2. 3PL

3PLs and new warehouses

Cin7 Core talking straight to the warehouse API.

07

Prestige skincare brand

Cin7 Core wired into a new 3PL's WMS API

  1. Cin7 Core
  2. AceForce connector
  3. PFC WMS API
Starting point
The brand was implementing Cin7 Core while moving to a 3PL whose WMS had no ready connector, with retailer EDI orders in the mix.
What we built
Chose to build instead of buying middleware. Specified and built five operations against the 3PL's WMS API: outbound orders, bulk order status with tracking, an hourly pull of retailer EDI orders, inbound ASNs and receiving results, plus a retailer to brand SKU translation.
Result
  • Orders, receipts and tracking in one loop
  • No middleware subscription
