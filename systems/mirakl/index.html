01

Oral care brand

Launch day on a prestige beauty retailer's marketplace

  2. Shopify
  3. Datex FootPrint WMS
Starting point
The brand needed to go live on a national beauty retailer's Mirakl marketplace with Shopify as its store.

02

Beauty and home brand

Department store marketplace orders into NetSuite

  1. Mirakl
  2. AceForce connector
  3. NetSuite
  4. Capacity
Starting point
Marketplace dropship orders needed to reach NetSuite and the 3PL without manual entry.
What we built
Built a connector that pulls orders awaiting acceptance, posts them to NetSuite with commission and trade discount mapping, sends fulfillments back and restricts carriers to the codes the retailer accepts.
Result
  • Live orders shipping through NetSuite
  • Accounting trained on tax and commission reconciliation
