Case studies

01

Haircare brand

Amazon straight into the demand plan

  1. Amazon SP-API
  2. AceForce app
  3. Demand plan
Starting point
Planning ran on weekly CSV exports from Seller Central, and a paid integration with 20 configured flows had stopped running.
What we built
Built a read only Amazon app, authorized by the brand's primary user with no credentials shared, that pulls SKU level order history plus FBA and AWD inventory into the planning model. Never touches pricing, listings or customer data.
Result
  • Sales velocity and on-hand stock refresh automatically
  • The unused integration was not renewed

03

Fragrance brand

FBA through Cin7 Core and the 3PL

  1. Amazon
  2. Cin7 Core
  3. 3PL
Starting point
The brand was launching on Amazon with Cin7 Core and a US 3PL already in place.
What we built
Set up a virtual FBA location in Cin7 Core, moved the launch SKUs onto it, connected the Amazon store and arranged FNSKU labeling at the 3PL.
Result
  • FBA stock tracked in Cin7 Core like any other location
04

Beauty brands

FBA replenishment that keeps cost of goods clean

  1. NetSuite / Cin7
  2. AceForce connector
  3. 3PL
  4. Amazon FBA
Starting point
Sending stock to Amazon from a 3PL left no clean inventory or cost trail.
What we built
Designed and built replenishment as a sales order to the Amazon ship-to that syncs to the 3PL and restocks into an Amazon location, plus a NetSuite to FBA flow for another brand.
Result
  • FBA shipments with a full inventory and cost of goods trail
