Global cosmetics brand

Tracking back into TikTok Shop after a 3PL move

  1. 3PL
  2. Shopify
  3. AceForce mapping
  4. TikTok Shop

What we built
Mapped the 3PL's carrier services so tracking was accepted and supplied a file of every fulfillment for bulk upload.

  • Tracking visible in Seller Center and linking to the carrier
  • Backlog loaded in bulk
