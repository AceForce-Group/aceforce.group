Inventory · Everything else we run

        
The connector is the part people see. Most of our work is the operating layer around it: planning, warehouses, vendors, launches and the people who run them.

      

      

        

          01♠Planning and forecasting+
          
  • Rolling 12 to 18 month demand forecasts by SKU, from finished goods to components
  • Integrated S&OP design and a monthly cycle: demand review, supply review, executive decision
  • Retailer unit forecasts by SKU built on target weeks of supply
  • Weekly purchasing plans and SKU level buy plans
  • Safety stock, reorder points and supplier lead time trackers
  • Reorder rules and supply reviews ready for CFO approval
+6 more

        

        

          02♦Supply chain and logistics+
          
  • 3PL RFPs: shortlist, bid normalization, scoring and negotiation
  • Migration cost and payback analysis, including telling you to stay put
  • Incumbent 3PL renegotiation with SLAs and improvement plans
  • 3PL site visits and audits: temperature control, FIFO, expiry
  • 3PL migrations: cutover plan, SKU mapping, inventory transfer, 30 day stabilization
  • Warehouse decisions: renew, relocate, 3PL or hybrid, with cost to serve by channel
+12 more

        

        

          03♣Vendors and manufacturing+
          
  • Manufacturer and supplier sourcing, vetting and quote comparison
  • Manufacturer management: schedules, MOQs, scorecards and single source risk
  • Manufacturer escalations to lock delivery dates
  • Formula ownership and IP verification
  • 3PL contract compliance audits and rate increase reviews against the MSA
  • Vendor billing disputes documented to the timestamp
+1 more

        

        

          04♥Product and packaging+
          
  • Packaging artwork to manufacturer specs and dieline fixes
  • Label compliance reviews
  • Product briefs and development
  • SKU, UPC, GS1 and item master setup for launch
  • Packaging inserts and gift with purchase planning
  • Bills of materials for domestic production
+1 more

        

        

          05♠Finance+
          
  • Landed cost trackers and processes
  • Cash flow and margin forecasts, including 14 week cash models
  • Monthly cash models and month end reviews with outside accountants
  • Unit economics and SKU P&Ls built from real invoices
  • A running P&L when the books cannot be trusted yet
  • Capital raise support: deck input and investor introductions
+5 more

        

        

          06♦Retail and channel+
          
  • Retail launch readiness: weeks of supply, ramp up and ship notice analysis
  • Retailer onboarding and compliance for Ulta, Nordstrom, Sephora and Target
  • Marketplace strategy across Target Plus, Amazon and TikTok Shop
  • Channel strategy for new brands
  • Influencer sampling and partnership introductions
  • Hospitality channel development

        

        

          07♣Organization and process+
          
  • SOPs, runbooks and process maps
  • Change management and coaching for planning teams
  • Role design and job descriptions
  • New hire onboarding with tool handover and a tool register
  • Retiring legacy planning tools
  • Ops team capacity assessments and roadmaps
+4 more

        

        

          08♥Leadership+
